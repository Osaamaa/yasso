Universe Pixel — original font artwork

The bitmap letterforms in Universe Pixel were drawn specifically for Yasso's
Gaming Universe. They are original project artwork, not copied from an existing
font, game, brand, or third-party font collection.

The letterforms, generated universe-pixel.ttf, and reproducible generator
tools/create_pixel_font.py are dedicated to the public domain under the
CC0 1.0 Universal dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may use, modify, embed, copy, and redistribute them for any purpose.
They are provided without warranty.

No third-party font or font-generation library is used. The generator uses only
the Python standard library. The website serves the completed font locally and
has no external font service, request, or runtime dependency.
