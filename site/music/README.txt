OPTIONAL BACKGROUND MUSIC

Put an MP3 named song.mp3 in this folder.
The website starts it only after PRESS START, loops it with a gentle fade-in,
and pauses it while a video is playing. The always-visible sound button
controls music, sound effects, and video sound together.

No music is included. If song.mp3 is missing or cannot be played, the gift
still works. Use music that you have permission to share if publishing.

You can use another filename by editing the music path in ../content.js.
